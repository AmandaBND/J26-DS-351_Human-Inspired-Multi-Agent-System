# Metropolitan Complaint Resolution Guidelines

STATUS: Prototype guidelines only.
These are proposed rules, not approved company policies.
A manager must review recommended actions.

## Equipment Breakdown
- Verify the equipment identification and service history.
- Check whether an active service request exists.
- Request technical diagnosis from the service team.
- Consider temporary alternatives for critical operations.
- Do not promise a repair completion time without confirmation.

## Service Delay
- Check the original complaint and service request dates.
- Verify technician assignment and visit status.
- Contact the responsible service coordinator.
- Escalate repeated or overdue service requests.
- Inform the customer of the verified next action.

## Rejected Service Job
- Retrieve the rejected job record.
- Identify the documented rejection reason.
- Verify technician remarks and job status.
- Refer unresolved cases to the team leader.
- Do not automatically reverse a rejection.

## AMC Issue
- Verify the Annual Maintenance Contract status.
- Check contractual service coverage.
- Review service obligations and exclusions.
- Escalate disputed coverage to the authorized team.

## Installation Issue
- Verify installation details and service records.
- Assign the appropriate installation support team.
- Review reported defects or incomplete work.

## Product Issue
- Verify product identification and warranty status.
- Review reported symptoms and previous service history.
- Refer technical faults to the appropriate support team.

## Priority and Escalation
- High priority: proposed initial response target 24 hours.
- Medium priority: proposed initial response target 48 hours.
- Low priority: proposed initial response target 72 hours.
- These targets require company approval.
- Safety concerns require prompt human assessment.

## Customer Communication
- Acknowledge the customer's concern professionally.
- Avoid blaming the customer or technician.
- Do not promise unconfirmed actions.
- Do not claim a technician has been assigned unless verified.
- Customer messages require human approval before sending.
