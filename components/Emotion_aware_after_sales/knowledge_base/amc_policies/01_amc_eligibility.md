# AMC-POL-001: Eligibility

Status: SYNTHETIC RESEARCH POLICY
Version: 1.0
Applies to: Printers and copiers

An Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) is an optional
annual service agreement for an eligible printer or copier.

For this research prototype, AMC eligibility normally
begins after the manufacturer's warranty has expired.

An AMC must be associated with a specific customer
and registered device.

AMC coverage dates must be verified against the
contract record in the database.
