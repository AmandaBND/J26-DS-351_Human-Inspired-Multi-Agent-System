# AMC-POL-002: Service Plans

Status: SYNTHETIC RESEARCH POLICY
Version: 1.0
Applies to: Printers and copiers

A demonstration AMC may include either three or four
preventive maintenance services per contract year.

Three-service plans generally schedule maintenance
approximately every four months.

Four-service plans generally schedule maintenance
approximately every three months.

Illustrative annual contract prices are LKR 15,000
and LKR 20,000.

These prices are demonstration values, not verified
Metropolitan Technologies prices.

The actual number of services and contract value
must come from the customer's contract record.
