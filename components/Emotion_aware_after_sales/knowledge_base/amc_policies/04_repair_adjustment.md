# AMC-POL-004: Repair and Maintenance Adjustment

Status: SYNTHETIC RESEARCH POLICY
Version: 1.0
Applies to: Printers and copiers

A completed customer-requested repair may satisfy
an upcoming scheduled AMC maintenance visit.

For the prototype, the repair must occur no more
than seven calendar days before the scheduled visit.

The repair and scheduled visit must concern the
same device and customer.

A technician must confirm that the required
preventive maintenance checks were completed
during the repair visit.

The service team must approve the substitution.

Without confirmation and approval, the system
must return MANUAL REVIEW REQUIRED rather than
automatically counting the AMC visit as completed.

Example:
Repair completed: 22 October 2026.
AMC visit scheduled: 24 October 2026.

The two-day difference satisfies the demonstration
time-window condition, but technician confirmation
and approval are still required.
