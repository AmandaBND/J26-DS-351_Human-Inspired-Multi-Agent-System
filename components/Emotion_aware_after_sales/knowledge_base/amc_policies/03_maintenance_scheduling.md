# AMC-POL-003: Maintenance Scheduling

Status: SYNTHETIC RESEARCH POLICY
Version: 1.0
Applies to: Printers and copiers

Scheduled maintenance dates depend on the number
of preventive services included in the AMC.

A completed service must be recorded against the
correct customer and device.

A scheduled visit must not be marked completed
unless there is evidence of the actual service.

Missed or delayed visits should be flagged for
review by the service team.
