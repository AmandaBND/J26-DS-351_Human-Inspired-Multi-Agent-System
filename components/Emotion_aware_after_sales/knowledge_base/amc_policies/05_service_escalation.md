# AMC-POL-005: Service Escalation

Status: SYNTHETIC RESEARCH POLICY
Version: 1.0
Applies to: Printers and copiers

Repeated printer or copier breakdowns should be
flagged for technical review.

Unresolved customer service requests require
follow-up by the service team.

Delayed AMC visits should be visible in the
service monitoring dashboard.

Negative customer emotion detected from a call
may be used as supporting evidence for prioritizing
human follow-up.

Emotion predictions must not independently determine
contract eligibility, billing, or service entitlements.

The prototype must distinguish confirmed database
facts from experimental emotion model predictions.
